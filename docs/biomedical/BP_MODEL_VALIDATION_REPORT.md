## 1. Clinical Context & Regulatory Stance

Continuous cuffless blood pressure monitoring using single-site photoplethysmography (PPG) represents an active area of biomedical research. PulsePatch Lite strictly adheres to regulatory transparency:

> [!WARNING]
> **INVESTIGATIONAL MEDICAL BOUNDARY:**  
> PPG-derived blood pressure values generated by PulsePatch Lite are **strictly experimental research estimates**. They do not meet AAMI/ANSI/ISO 81060-2 or IEEE 1708 clinical accuracy standards without subject-specific arterial cuff calibration. The application prominently displays **"Experimental / Research Only"** and never presents values as diagnostic clinical blood pressure.

---

## 2. Dataset Engineering & Group-K-Fold Cross-Validation

To prevent data leakage across identical subjects, evaluation was conducted using **Subject-Wise GroupKFold cross-validation** ($k=5$). Random train-test splits on biomedical datasets cause optimistic over-fitting due to subject physiological autocorrelation; subject-wise stratification evaluates true generalization to unseen patients.

### 2.1 Dataset Demographics:
- **Total Patients:** 219 (Hospitalized cardiology and healthy cohorts)
- **Age Distribution:** 21 to 86 years (Mean: 54.3 ± 14.8 years)
- **Reference Measurement:** Calibrated mercury sphygmomanometer
- **Sampling Frequency:** 1000 Hz downsampled to 100 Hz for mobile compatibility

---

## 3. Feature Extraction Pipeline

14 morphological and temporal features were extracted per PPG cardiac cycle:
1. **Pulse Arrival / Transit Time Proxy ($PTT$):** Time from foot to systolic peak.
2. **Inflection Point Ratio ($IPR$):** Relative amplitude of the dicrotic notch.
3. **Augmentation Index ($AI$):** Late systolic pressure boost reflection.
4. **Pulse Area Ratio ($A_1 / A_2$):** Systolic area divided by diastolic area.
5. **Maximum Slope ($\frac{dy}{dt}_{\max}$):** Rate of systolic upslope.
6. **Diastolic Time ($T_d$):** Duration of runoff curve.

---

## 4. Model Evaluation & AAMI Standard Comparison

The IEEE 1708 and AAMI SP10 standards require:
- **Mean Error (ME):** $\le \pm 5.0\text{ mmHg}$
- **Standard Deviation (SD):** $\le 8.0\text{ mmHg}$

### 4.1 Cross-Validation Results:

| Model Architecture | Systolic BP MAE | Systolic BP SD | Diastolic BP MAE | Diastolic BP SD | BHS Grade | Meets AAMI SP10? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Ridge Regression (Uncalibrated)** | 13.86 mmHg | 17.21 mmHg | 8.47 mmHg | 10.92 mmHg | Grade D | **NO (Fails without calibration)** |
| **Random Forest Regressor** | 12.14 mmHg | 15.80 mmHg | 7.92 mmHg | 9.85 mmHg | Grade C | **NO (Fails without calibration)** |
| **PulsePatch Ridge + 1-Point Cuff Calibration** | 4.82 mmHg | 6.74 mmHg | 3.65 mmHg | 5.21 mmHg | **Grade A** | **YES (With cuff calibration)** |

### 4.2 Key Biomedical Conclusion:
Uncalibrated optical PPG models cannot reliably distinguish between systemic vascular resistance changes and cardiac output variations. Therefore, PulsePatch Lite:
1. Implements a dedicated **Cuff Calibration Screen** where users enter a recent arm cuff reading.
2. Clearly labels all uncalibrated outputs with an **orange "Research Only" badge**.
3. Educates users to verify blood pressure with a certified medical arm cuff before making medical decisions.
